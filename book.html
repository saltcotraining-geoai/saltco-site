The Book

Digital Independence & Applied Spatial Analysis Using QGIS

A two-floor book for self-learners and surveyors. Floor One rebuilds your computing with free, open-source software on the hardware you already own: why open source, Linux on a 2–4 GB laptop, the live USB, the terminal, and your first year as a Linux user. Floor Two is the complete Applied Spatial Analysis Using QGIS course — field data, metres, topology, queries, buffers, rasters, print layouts, offline web maps, and the capstone.

Text licence CC BY 4.0. Written in Quarto, rendered to HTML, PDF and EPUB, and built from the weekly session write-ups so the book and the course cannot drift apart. Every example runs offline on QGIS 3.44 and open data.

Status: in development. Floor Two is fully drafted through the capstone (chapters 16, 20, 21, 22 are written; 13–15 and 17–19 are next from their kits). Floor One and the trainer chapters are scaffolded.

Read it now: every chapter below links to the published session write-up — the raw material the book chapter is being built from, with the same timings, data and failures.
