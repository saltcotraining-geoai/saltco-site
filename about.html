About saltco

Who We Are

saltco is a one-person movement for digital independence. Founded by Sallah Yaya — Senior GIS Analyst, Surveying Engineer (18+ years), and Google AI Fundamentals certified — saltco teaches open-source geospatial computing and AI to people with limited resources, so that skill and talent, not money, determine who gets to work with technology.

The Mission

Every lesson must work offline. Every tool must be open-source. Every student should be able to teach the next one. That is capacity building.

Licensing
